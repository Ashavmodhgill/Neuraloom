Inside NeuralArt

Creative control,
powered by AI

Blend the structure of your photograph with the visual character of a style image. NeuralArt makes neural style transfer approachable, adjustable, and ready to explore.

Made for creative exploration

AdaIN style transfer

Adaptive Instance Normalization aligns feature statistics from your style image with the content representation to create a new stylized image.

Adjustable style strength

Use the style-strength control to blend the stylized features with the original content features, from the original appearance to full style transfer.

Encoder + decoder pipeline

A pretrained VGG encoder extracts image features, and the trained decoder turns the blended representation back into an image.

GPU or CPU inference

When CUDA is available the model uses it for inference; otherwise it runs on the CPU, so a dedicated GPU is not required to try the app.

Common image formats

Upload PNG, JPG, or JPEG images as your content and style references. The generated result can be viewed and downloaded.

Trainable decoder

The included training script lets you train a decoder with your own content and style image folders and save experiment checkpoints.
